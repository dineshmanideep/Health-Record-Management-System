# Hospital Role – Functional Description

---

# 1. Overview of the Hospital Role

The hospital represents a healthcare institution within the platform.

Hospitals are responsible for managing the relationship between the institution and healthcare professionals such as doctors and medical assistants (nurses).

Unlike earlier designs where hospitals directly created doctor and nurse accounts, in this system **doctors and nurses maintain their own independent profiles**.

Hospitals do not create these profiles. Instead, hospitals **associate existing professionals with their institution using a unique hospital code**.

This approach allows healthcare professionals to maintain a **single professional identity**, even if they change hospitals in the future.

Hospitals therefore act as **organizational managers**, responsible for linking and managing healthcare staff who operate under their institution.

Hospitals **do not have access to patient medical records**.

Their role is strictly administrative and focused on **staff management and institutional organization**.

---

# 2. Hospital Registration

Hospitals must first create an account on the platform.

During registration, the hospital provides institutional details such as:

Hospital name
Email address
Contact phone number
Hospital address
Medical license or registration number

After submitting the registration form, the hospital account is created but remains **inactive until it is approved by the system administrator**.

This approval process ensures that only legitimate healthcare institutions can operate within the platform.

---

# 3. Admin Approval

To prevent unauthorized institutions from entering the platform, hospital accounts must go through an **admin approval process**.

The process works as follows:

1. The hospital submits the registration form.
2. The system marks the account status as **Pending**.
3. The administrator reviews the hospital registration information.
4. The administrator either **approves or rejects** the request.

Only approved hospitals can log in and access the hospital dashboard.

---

# 4. Hospital Login

After the hospital account is approved, the hospital administrator can log in using the registered credentials.

Once logged in, the hospital is redirected to the **Hospital Dashboard**.

The hospital dashboard focuses on **managing healthcare professionals associated with the hospital**.

---

# 5. Hospital Dashboard Overview

The hospital dashboard provides tools for managing institutional staff relationships.

Typical sections of the hospital dashboard include:

Hospital profile
Hospital code management
Connected doctors
Connected nurses
Doctor–nurse assignments
Audit logs

The dashboard should clearly display all healthcare professionals currently associated with the hospital.

---

# 6. Hospital Code System

Each hospital can **generate a unique hospital code**.

This hospital code is used by doctors and nurses to request association with the hospital.

Hospitals may generate different codes depending on the purpose, for example:

Doctor joining code
Nurse joining code

These codes act as **secure identifiers** that allow healthcare professionals to connect to the hospital.

The hospital administrator may regenerate or invalidate codes when necessary.

---

# 7. Doctor and Nurse Profiles

Doctors and nurses create **their own professional accounts** on the platform.

These profiles are independent of hospitals.

A professional profile may contain information such as:

Name
Email
Role (Doctor or Nurse)
Specialization (for doctors)

Because these profiles are independent, a healthcare professional can maintain the same identity even if they move to a different hospital.

This design prevents duplicate accounts and maintains consistent professional identity.

---

# 8. Joining a Hospital

Doctors and nurses can join a hospital by using the hospital code provided by the institution.

The joining process works as follows:

1. The doctor or nurse logs into their account.
2. The professional enters the hospital code.
3. The system sends a **join request** to the hospital.
4. The hospital administrator reviews the request.
5. The hospital either **accepts or rejects** the request.

If the request is approved, the doctor or nurse becomes **associated with that hospital**.

This association allows them to participate in hospital workflows such as consultations and prescription digitization.

---

# 9. Connected Staff List

The hospital dashboard should display a list of all healthcare professionals currently associated with the hospital.

This list should include:

Doctors connected to the hospital
Nurses connected to the hospital

For each staff member, the hospital should be able to see basic information such as:

Name
Role
Specialization (for doctors)
Connection status

This section helps the hospital keep track of all professionals operating under its institution.

---

# 10. Assigning Nurses to Doctors

In real-world healthcare environments, nurses often work under specific doctors or departments.

The platform should allow hospitals to assign nurses to doctors.

This assignment defines which nurse assists which doctor when creating patient treatment records.

Hospitals should be able to perform the following actions:

Assign a nurse to a doctor
Change nurse assignments
Remove nurse assignments

These relationships help organize the workflow for creating prescriptions and medical records.

---

# 11. Revoking Hospital Association

Hospitals must have the ability to remove healthcare professionals from their institution.

This may happen in situations such as:

A doctor leaving the hospital
A nurse being transferred
A professional contract ending

The hospital administrator can revoke the association between the professional and the hospital.

When this happens:

The professional account still exists in the platform.

However, the professional is **no longer associated with that hospital**.

This allows professionals to continue working in the system while hospitals maintain control over institutional membership.

---

# 12. Hospital Restrictions

Hospitals do not have access to patient medical records.

Hospitals cannot:

View patient health data
Access prescriptions
Download patient reports

Hospitals are responsible only for **staff management**, not for handling patient medical information.

This rule protects patient privacy and ensures that medical data remains accessible only to authorized healthcare professionals.

---

# 13. Audit Logging

All administrative actions performed by the hospital must be recorded in the system's **audit log**.

This ensures transparency and accountability.

Examples of actions that should be logged include:

Doctor joined hospital
Nurse joined hospital
Nurse assigned to doctor
Hospital revoked doctor association
Hospital revoked nurse association

Each log entry should contain the following information:

Action performed
User performing the action
Timestamp
Additional details

Example log entry:

Doctor association approved
Hospital: Yashoda Hospital
Doctor: Dr. Ravi
Time: 10:30 AM

These logs help maintain a clear record of administrative activities within the hospital.

---

# 14. Role of Hospitals in the Overall System

Hospitals act as the **organizational layer** within the platform.

Their main purpose is to manage relationships between healthcare professionals and the institution.

Hospitals ensure that doctors and nurses operate within a legitimate organizational structure.

However, hospitals do not interact directly with medical records.

Patient data remains controlled by patients and accessed only by authorized doctors.

This design ensures a balance between **institutional management and patient privacy**.
